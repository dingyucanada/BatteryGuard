# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Avoid inflated scientific, safety, or commercial claims.
- Protect confidential datasets, credentials, and hidden evaluation labels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this code. Report conduct issues privately to the repository owner through the contact information on their GitHub profile.
